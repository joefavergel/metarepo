# One-pager

Fits one page (about 300-450 words, unless the operator sets a length). One idea, readable in two minutes.

- Open with the summary; every key point is a weight-bearing statement and carries a footnote to a wiki page.
- Prefer three to five key points. Cut anything the wiki does not state.
- Next steps only when a wiki page supports them; otherwise omit the section.
- Footnotes list wiki pages as relative links. Do not cite originals unless the operator asked for them.
