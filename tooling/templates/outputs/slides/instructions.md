# Slides

One idea per slide, at most five short bullets; slide titles state the point. Slides are separated by `---`.

- Weight-bearing slides cite a wiki page; a final "Sources" slide lists every page used as a relative link.
- Put detail in speaker notes (`<!-- ... -->`) rather than on the slide.
- Marp format keeps the `marp: true` frontmatter; for markdown or html format drop it.
