# Report

A structured, multi-section document. Organize by theme or question, not by wiki page.

- Executive summary last-written, first-shown; it repeats nothing the body does not support.
- Each section cites the wiki pages it draws on; weight-bearing statements carry their own footnote.
- "Limitations and open questions" lists what the wiki does not cover (the "Not covered" items) and any recorded
  contradiction, attributed.
- Keep the section count to what the material supports; do not pad.
